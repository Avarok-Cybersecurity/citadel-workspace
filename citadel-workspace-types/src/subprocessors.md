# Citadel Workspace Subprocessors

**Effective October 14, 2026**

Avarok Corp ("Avarok") uses the subprocessors below to provide Citadel Workspace. Each is bound by a written agreement that requires it to protect personal data and to process it only to provide its services to Avarok.

| Subprocessor | Purpose | Data processed | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cloudflare, Inc.** | Hosting of the web application and of each workspace's server (Workers, Durable Objects, and storage), the control-plane database, network delivery and security, relays for connections between members (TURN), bot protection (Turnstile), and transactional email | All Customer Data stored on workspace servers, account and workspace metadata, network and connection data, and email addresses for service email. End-to-end encrypted content passes through relays only in encrypted form. | United States and Cloudflare's global network |
| **Stripe, Inc.** | Payment processing, subscriptions, invoicing, and the billing portal | Billing contact details, payment method details (held by Stripe; Avarok does not receive full card numbers), and subscription and payment history | United States |

## Changes to this list

Avarok will give at least **30 days' notice** before authorizing a new subprocessor to process Customer Data, by updating this page and notifying the workspace's account contact where the Data Processing Addendum requires it. A Customer may object as described in the Data Processing Addendum.

To request notifications or ask about this list, email security@avarok.net.
