# Citadel Workspace Data Processing Addendum

**Effective October 14, 2026**

This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") forms part of the Citadel Workspace Customer Terms (the "Agreement") between **Avarok Corp**, a Delaware corporation ("Avarok"), and the Customer. It applies when Avarok processes Customer Personal Data on Customer's behalf. Capitalized terms not defined here have the meanings given in the Agreement. If this DPA conflicts with the Agreement, this DPA governs as to the processing of personal data.

## 1. Definitions

- **"Data Protection Laws"** means all laws that apply to the processing of Customer Personal Data under the Agreement, including, as applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation 2016/679 ("GDPR"), the UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018, the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection, and U.S. state privacy laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended ("CCPA").
- **"Customer Personal Data"** means personal data within Customer Data that Avarok processes on Customer's behalf.
- **"controller", "processor", "data subject", "personal data", "processing", "supervisory authority"**, and **"Personal Data Breach"** have the meanings given in the GDPR. **"Business", "service provider", "sell"**, and **"share"** have the meanings given in the CCPA.
- **"Subprocessor"** means a third party Avarok engages to process Customer Personal Data.
- **"SCCs"** means the standard contractual clauses approved by European Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914.

## 2. Roles and instructions

1. Customer is the controller (or a processor acting for its own controllers), and Avarok is the processor of Customer Personal Data. Under the CCPA, Customer is the business and Avarok is its service provider.
2. Avarok will process Customer Personal Data only on Customer's documented instructions, unless required otherwise by law, in which case Avarok will inform Customer before processing unless the law prohibits it. The Agreement, this DPA, and Customer's use and configuration of the Service, including the settings its Admins choose, are Customer's complete instructions.
3. Avarok will inform Customer if, in its opinion, an instruction infringes Data Protection Laws.
4. Customer is responsible for the lawfulness of its instructions and for having all notices, consents, and legal bases needed for the processing.
5. The subject matter, nature, purpose, duration, and categories of data and data subjects are described in **Annex I**.

## 3. End-to-end encrypted content

Messages and files exchanged directly between Members' agents are encrypted end to end, including when relayed by Avarok's infrastructure, and Avarok does not hold the keys. Avarok's processing of that content is limited to transmitting it in encrypted form. Avarok cannot access, correct, export, or delete it, and obligations in this DPA that require access to content apply only to Customer Personal Data that Avarok can access.

## 4. Avarok personnel and support access

Avarok will ensure that personnel authorized to process Customer Personal Data are bound by confidentiality obligations. Avarok reads diagnostic logs stored in a workspace **only while an Admin has granted Avarok support access**, for up to 30 days per grant, and only to diagnose and support the Service. Each such access is recorded in a log visible to Customer's Admins.

## 5. Security

Avarok will implement and maintain appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect Customer Personal Data, as described in **Annex II**. Avarok may update these measures if the update does not materially reduce the overall level of protection.

## 6. Subprocessors

1. Customer generally authorizes Avarok to engage Subprocessors. The current list is at https://work.avarok.net/legal/subprocessors.
2. Avarok will impose data protection obligations on each Subprocessor that are no less protective than those in this DPA, and remains liable for each Subprocessor's performance.
3. Avarok will give Customer at least 30 days' notice before a new Subprocessor processes Customer Personal Data. Customer may object on reasonable data protection grounds within that period. The parties will discuss the objection in good faith. If they cannot resolve it, Customer may terminate the affected part of the Service and receive a refund of prepaid fees for the unused period, as its sole remedy.

## 7. Data subject requests and assistance

Taking into account the nature of the processing, Avarok will provide the Service's features, such as the ability to edit profiles, remove Members, and delete diagnostic logs, and reasonable further assistance, to help Customer respond to data subject requests. If Avarok receives a request directly, it will refer the data subject to Customer where it can identify Customer. Avarok will provide reasonable assistance with Customer's data protection impact assessments and consultations with supervisory authorities, to the extent required by Data Protection Laws.

## 8. Personal Data Breaches

Avarok will notify Customer **without undue delay, and where feasible within 72 hours**, after becoming aware of a Personal Data Breach affecting Customer Personal Data on systems Avarok controls. The notice will include the information reasonably available to Avarok to help Customer meet its own obligations. Avarok will take reasonable steps to contain and remedy the breach. Notification is not an acknowledgment of fault.

## 9. Deletion and return

On termination of the Agreement, Avarok will make Customer Personal Data stored on the workspace server available for export for 30 days, as described in the Agreement, and will then delete it, unless the law requires Avarok to keep it. Data in backups is deleted under Avarok's normal backup rotation and remains protected by this DPA until then.

## 10. Audits

Avarok will make available to Customer the information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA, including responses to reasonable security questionnaires and any third-party audit reports or certifications Avarok holds. If that information is insufficient to meet Customer's obligations under Data Protection Laws, or a supervisory authority requires it, Customer may conduct an audit at its own expense:

- no more than once in any 12-month period;
- on at least 30 days' written notice;
- during business hours, without unreasonably disrupting Avarok's operations; and
- subject to confidentiality obligations.

## 11. International transfers

1. Avarok may process Customer Personal Data in the United States and wherever its Subprocessors operate.
2. To the extent Customer Personal Data originating in the European Economic Area is transferred to a country without an adequacy decision, the parties agree to the **SCCs**, which are incorporated by reference. Module 2 (controller to processor) applies where Customer is a controller, and Module 3 (processor to processor) where Customer is a processor.
3. Under the SCCs:
   - Clause 7 (docking) applies;
   - Clause 9 option 2 applies, with the notice period in Section 6.3;
   - the optional language in Clause 11 does not apply;
   - Clauses 17 and 18 are governed by and resolved in the courts of Ireland;
   - Annexes I and II of this DPA complete the SCCs' Annexes I and II, and the Subprocessor list completes Annex III.
4. For transfers from the United Kingdom, the International Data Transfer Addendum to the SCCs issued by the UK Information Commissioner applies, with this DPA's details completing its tables, and either party may end it as described in its Section 19.
5. For transfers from Switzerland, the SCCs apply with references to the GDPR read as references to the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection, and the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner as the competent authority.
6. If a transfer mechanism is invalidated, the parties will cooperate to put an alternative in place.

## 12. CCPA service provider terms

Avarok will not:

- sell or share Customer Personal Data;
- retain, use, or disclose it for any purpose other than the business purposes specified in the Agreement, or outside the direct business relationship with Customer;
- combine it with personal information Avarok receives from other sources, except as the CCPA permits.

Avarok will comply with the CCPA's obligations that apply to service providers and provide the same level of privacy protection the CCPA requires of businesses. It will notify Customer if it can no longer meet those obligations. Customer may take reasonable steps to stop and remedy unauthorized use of Customer Personal Data.

## 13. Liability and term

Each party's liability under this DPA is subject to the limitations of liability in the Agreement, except where Data Protection Laws or the SCCs do not permit those limits. This DPA lasts as long as Avarok processes Customer Personal Data under the Agreement.

## Annex I. Description of the processing

- **Data exporter:** Customer, as identified at workspace creation. Its contact is its account email.
- **Data importer:** Avarok Corp, 251 Little Falls Drive, Wilmington, DE 19808, United States, as processor. Contact: security@avarok.net.
- **Data subjects:** Customer's Admins and Members, and people whose information Members include in content stored in the workspace.
- **Categories of personal data:**
  - account identifiers (usernames, display names, numeric account IDs);
  - optional profile details (email address, title, avatar);
  - roles and permissions;
  - content posted to shared workspace channels and storage;
  - diagnostic log lines Members choose to share, redacted as described in the Terms of Use;
  - connection and network data needed to operate the Service.
- **Special categories:** none intended. Customer will not submit special categories of data unless agreed in writing.
- **Frequency:** continuous, for the term of the Agreement.
- **Nature and purpose:**
  - hosting, storage, transmission, and relaying to provide the Service;
  - security and abuse prevention;
  - support at Customer's request or under an Admin's support grant.
- **Duration and retention:** the term of the Agreement plus the 30-day export period. Diagnostic logs are kept for the retention period Customer's Admins set, up to 90 days.
- **Subprocessor transfers:** as listed at https://work.avarok.net/legal/subprocessors, for the purposes stated there.

## Annex II. Technical and organizational security measures

1. **Encryption:** end-to-end, post-quantum encryption for messages and files exchanged between Members' agents; encrypted connections between agents, browsers, and workspace servers; storage encrypted at rest by Avarok's infrastructure provider.
2. **Authentication:** sign-in protocols designed so that passwords are not sent to or stored by Avarok's servers in readable form; support for passkeys; bot protection on sign-up and sign-in where enabled.
3. **Isolation:** each workspace runs in its own isolated server instance with its own storage.
4. **Access control:** role-based permissions within each workspace. Avarok's access to diagnostic logs only under an Admin's time-limited grant, verified by post-quantum digital signatures and recorded in an audit log visible to Admins. Least-privilege access to production systems for Avarok personnel.
5. **Data minimization:** diagnostic logs are optional, off by default, and redacted on the Member's device before sending. They are size-limited and expire automatically.
6. **Software integrity:** Citadel Agent releases are signed with post-quantum (ML-DSA) and platform code-signing signatures, and updates are verified before installation.
7. **Monitoring and response:** service monitoring, abuse prevention, and an incident response process with breach notification under Section 8.
8. **Resilience:** hosting on a globally distributed provider with redundancy.
9. **Deletion:** deletion of workspace data after the export period; Members can delete their own diagnostic logs at any time.
10. **Vendor management:** written data protection terms with each Subprocessor.
